About the Project

Tracking What the World Cares About

PopFlow aggregates trend signals from multiple data sources — social media APIs, news feeds, and search trend data — into a unified MongoDB pipeline. The platform normalizes and scores trends across domains in near real time.

The ETL pipeline features automated ingestion schedules, deduplication logic, and error recovery across 100K+ records.

The trend scoring engine uses weighted signals to surface emerging topics before they peak.
