What I built

Traditional risk models assume normal distributions and symmetric market reactions — but real markets don't work that way. Negative news triggers exponentially stronger panic than positive news triggers optimism. This project built a generative news-shock framework that maps daily sentiment signals directly to equity volatility, using a downside-asymmetric Student-t distribution to correctly model fat tails and stress periods. The business name for the project was Zanista.
