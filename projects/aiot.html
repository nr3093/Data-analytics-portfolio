Goal

Wearables track everything except how we feel. Draw Your Mood fills the gap — turning motion and sound into a real-time map of your emotional day.

Problem & solution

⚠ The Problem

Steps, sleep, and screen time are tracked — but emotional experience stays invisible. We lose sight of how our days actually felt, only that we were busy.

💡 The Solution

A real-time AIoT system that classifies emotion every 2 seconds from motion + sound, then reflects it back as a personal mood timeline you can read and share.

How it works for a user

1

Wear a small device

An M5Stack Core2 worn during the day senses motion (50Hz IMU) and sound (16kHz microphone) continuously.

2

Tap the screen to label your mood

Tap the touchscreen when you want to log how you feel — Calm, Energetic, Stressed, or Tired. These become ground-truth labels for the model.

3

System predicts mood every 2 seconds

Raw signals are turned into 11 engineered features, sent via MQTT to AWS IoT Core, and classified by an XGBoost model — with a prediction back on the device in under 2s.

4

View your day as a mood timeline

Open the Streamlit dashboard to see your day rendered as a colored mood timeline — and compare patterns across all 5 users.

→ From raw sensor signals to a personal story
