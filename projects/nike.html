About the Project

Data Meets Executive Storytelling

Nike's recent revenue decline presented a complex story buried in financial reports. This project makes that story impossible to ignore — and actionable for leadership.

I built three interlocking Tableau dashboards: a revenue trend overview, a regional performance breakdown, and a product-category analysis. Each answers one key executive question.

The 10-slide boardroom story synthesizes the dashboards into a narrative arc: what happened, why, and where the opportunity lies.
